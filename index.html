5 Minute Manager Football

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy explains what personal data the app 5 Minute Manager Football (package com.minutefootballmanager) processes, for what purpose, with whom it is shared and how you can exercise your rights.

1. Data controller

  • Controller: Daniel Ortiz Lausín (individual developer)
  • Contact: dortiz90@gmail.com

2. What data we process

2.1. Google Sign-In (optional)

The game can be played without an account. If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive and store:

  • Your Google account identifier
  • Your email address
  • Your name and the nickname you pick inside the game

We never receive your Google password, nor access to your email, contacts or other account data.

2.2. Game progress

Your saves (squads, line-ups, standings, in-game economy) are stored on your own device. If you sign in, they are also uploaded to our server so you can restore them on another device. These saves contain no real personal data, only game progress and your nickname.

2.3. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store card or billing details; we only receive confirmation of which product you bought so we can activate it.

2.4. Advertising

The free version shows rewarded ads, which only appear if you tap the corresponding button. They are served by Google AdMob, which may process the device advertising identifier, IP address and approximate device data for advertising and measurement purposes. In the European Union we ask for consent before showing personalised ads. If you buy the premium version, no ads are shown.

2.5. What we do NOT collect

The app does not request or collect precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, personal files, messages or health data.

3. Why we use the data and our legal basis

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for purposes other than those described.

4. Who we share it with

  • Google LLC (Google Sign-In, Google AdMob and Google Play Billing), under their policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Server hosting provider (Render), with servers located in the European Union (Frankfurt), where the game save backups are stored.

Any transfers outside the European Economic Area rely on those providers' contractual safeguards.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account and server backups: for as long as the account exists. If you request deletion, they are erased within a maximum of 30 days.
  • Saves on your device: until you delete them, either from the in-game settings (“Reset progress”) or by uninstalling the app.

6. Your rights and how to delete your data

You can request access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction or objection by writing to dortiz90@gmail.com from the email address you registered with. To delete your account and all backups stored on our server, send an email with the subject “Delete account”: it will be erased within a maximum of 30 days. You can also stop using your account at any time from “Settings → Account and cloud → Sign out”.

If you are in the European Union, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

7. Security

All communications with the server are encrypted using HTTPS, and access to your account is authenticated with a personal token that you can revoke by signing out.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children under that age. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you inside the game.

10. Contact

Any privacy question: dortiz90@gmail.com
