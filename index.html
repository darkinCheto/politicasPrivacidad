5 Minute Manager Football

Football manager game for Android

Package com.minutefootballmanager · Developed by Daniel Ortiz Lausín

5 Minute Manager Football is a football manager game: you take charge of a real club, pick your starting eleven, manage the squad and the money, and play complete seasons in a matter of minutes instead of hours.

What the app does

  • Manage a real club. Choose one of the hundreds of clubs and leagues included, or create your own club and climb the divisions season after season.
  • Pick your eleven and play. Choose the line-up, watch the matches of every matchday and follow the league table, the national cups and the continental competitions.
  • Transfers, youth academy and finances. Sign players from the market, bring youngsters through the academy and upgrade your facilities.
  • Several game modes. Manager, Draft, Create your club, Sports Director, Challenges, local multiplayer and an online league.
  • Plays offline. The game works without an account and without an internet connection.
  • Available in 13 languages, including Spanish, English, Portuguese, German, French, Italian, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Hindi, Arabic and Indonesian.

Signing in with Google and why we ask for it

The game can be played completely without signing in. Signing in with Google is optional and is used for one thing only: saving your game in the cloud so that you can continue your career on another device or restore it if you change phones.

To do that we receive only your Google account identifier, your email address and your name. We never receive your Google password and we do not access your email, your contacts or any other Google data. We do not use your Google data for advertising, we do not sell it and we do not share it with anyone other than the providers needed to run the service (Google itself and our hosting provider). You can read the full details in our Privacy Policy.

You can stop using your account and delete all the data stored on our server at any time from the game (Settings → Account and cloud → Sign out) or by writing to the contact address.

Resumen en español

5 Minute Manager Football es un juego de gestión de fútbol: fichas por un club real, eliges tu once, gestionas la plantilla y el presupuesto, y juegas temporadas completas en pocos minutos. Incluye varios modos de juego, academia de cantera, mercado de fichajes y liga online, y está disponible en 13 idiomas.

El juego se puede jugar sin iniciar sesión. El acceso con Google es opcional y sirve únicamente para guardar tu partida en la nube y poder continuarla en otro dispositivo. Solo recibimos el identificador de tu cuenta de Google, tu correo y tu nombre: nunca tu contraseña, ni acceso a tu correo o a tus contactos, y no usamos esos datos para publicidad. Puedes borrar tu cuenta y todos los datos guardados en el servidor desde Ajustes → Cuenta y nube → Cerrar sesión o escribiendo al correo de contacto.
